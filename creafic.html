The Practice

Explore my graphic design work

Creafic was an independent design practice built on one principle: creative work should solve a business problem, not just look good. From 2018 to 2021, I led every engagement from first brief to final delivery, producing brand identities, packaging, and campaign collateral across healthcare, nonprofit, education, retail, beauty, sports, and tourism.

The work demanded more than design skill. It called for high-level client service, disciplined campaign execution, and creative leadership: turning objectives into concepts, holding timelines, and delivering assets that performed across print, digital, and in-market channels.
