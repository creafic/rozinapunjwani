Creafic is an independent design practice. Since 2018, I lead every engagement from first brief to final delivery, producing brand identities, packaging, and campaign collateral across healthcare, nonprofit, education, retail, beauty, sports, and tourism.

The work demands more than design skill. It calls for high-level client service, disciplined campaign execution, and creative leadership: turning objectives into concepts, holding timelines, and delivering assets that perform across print, digital, and in-market channels.
